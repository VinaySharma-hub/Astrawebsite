Our Projects

Explore our journey through drones, innovation, and competitions.

Autonomous Drone

Our autonomous drone project focuses on AI-based navigation using computer vision. It can detect obstacles, follow paths, and adapt to changing environments, ensuring safety and efficiency during critical missions. Perfect for real-world applications!

Drone Workshop

We conducted an engaging workshop where students built drones from scratch. From assembling motors to flight calibration, participants explored hands-on drone mechanics and learned essential UAV technologies with teamwork and innovation.

Competition Highlights

Team Astra participated in inter-college drone racing events, showcasing speed, stability, and cutting-edge flight control. Our prototypes competed with top teams, proving our dedication towards UAV excellence and engineering innovation.

Team Astra

Meet our passionate engineers and innovators. Team Astra blends creativity, coding, and mechanical expertise to develop drones for multiple domains. Our mission: inspire, innovate, and revolutionize drone technology across industries.
